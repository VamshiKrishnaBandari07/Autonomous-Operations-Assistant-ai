OpsFlow AI — New Hire FAQ (Sample)

Q: When do I get my laptop?
A: IT provisions equipment before day one. Contact #it-onboarding if it has not arrived by your start date.

Q: How do I join Slack?
A: You receive a workspace invite as part of the accounts checklist during onboarding.

Q: Who is my first point of contact?
A: Your manager and assigned onboarding buddy. People Ops is available in #people-ops.

Q: What must I complete in week one?
A: HRIS profile, benefits enrollment start, security training, and orientation session attendance.

Q: Where are company policies?
A: Ask OpsFlow AI in chat, or browse the knowledge base documents uploaded by Operations.
