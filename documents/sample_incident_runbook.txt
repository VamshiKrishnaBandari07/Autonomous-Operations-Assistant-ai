OpsFlow AI — Incident Response Runbook (Sample)

Severity definitions
- Sev1: Customer-facing outage or data loss risk. Page on-call immediately.
- Sev2: Major degradation with workaround. Acknowledge within 15 minutes.
- Sev3: Limited impact. Triage during business hours.

Acknowledgement SLA
All production incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes of detection.
Sev1 incidents require an incident commander and a status update every 30 minutes.

Communication
- Post to #incidents within 5 minutes of acknowledgement.
- Update the public status page within 20 minutes for customer-facing outages.
- After resolution, schedule a blameless postmortem within 48 hours.

Rollback
If a change caused the incident, prefer rollback over forward-fix unless data integrity is at risk.
Hotfixes still require a peer review within 24 hours.
