OpsFlow AI — Sample Operations Policy

1. Incident Response
All production incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
Severity 1 incidents require an incident commander and a status update every 30 minutes.
Customer-facing outages must be communicated via the status page within 20 minutes.

2. Change Management
Production deployments require a peer review and approval from the on-call engineer.
Hotfixes may skip the standard CAB window but must include a post-change review within 48 hours.
Rollback plans are mandatory for all high-risk changes.

3. Access Control
Privileged access to production systems is granted via time-bound tickets only.
Shared admin accounts are prohibited.
Access reviews for critical systems are performed quarterly.

4. Task Ownership
Every operational task must have a named owner and a deadline.
Critical tasks without an owner for more than 4 hours are automatically escalated to the Operations Lead.
Task priority levels are: low, medium, high, critical.

5. Knowledge Management
Company policies and runbooks must be stored in OpsFlow AI for RAG-based retrieval.
Employees should ask OpsFlow before creating duplicate documentation.
Meeting action items must be captured in the task board within one business day.

6. Reporting
Weekly operational reports summarise open incidents, task backlog, and automation savings.
Bottlenecks are flagged when open critical tasks exceed five or when mean acknowledgement time exceeds SLA.
