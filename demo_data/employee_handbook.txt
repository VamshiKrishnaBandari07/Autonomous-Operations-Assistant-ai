1. Working Hours
Standard hours are 09:00–17:30 Monday to Friday with flexible start between 08:00 and 10:00.

2. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 25 annual leave days per calendar year, plus public holidays.
Part-time leave is pro-rated by contracted hours.
Leave requests should be submitted at least 10 working days in advance via the HR portal.
Managers must respond to leave requests within 3 business days.

3. Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 paid sick days per year with self-certification for absences of 3 days or fewer.
Absences longer than 3 days require a medical certificate.

4. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 local time.

5. Parental Leave
Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of parental leave as per company policy.
Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

6. Learning & Development
Each employee has a £1,000 annual learning budget.
Mandatory security awareness training must be completed within the first 30 days.

7. Code of Conduct
Treat colleagues with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.
Report concerns to People Ops or anonymously via the ethics hotline.
