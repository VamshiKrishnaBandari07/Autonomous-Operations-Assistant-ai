OpsFlow Technologies — Company Operations Policy (Demo)

1. Incident Response
All production incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
Severity 1 incidents require an incident commander and status updates every 30 minutes.
Customer-facing outages must be published on the status page within 20 minutes.

2. Change Management
Production changes require peer review and on-call approval.
Emergency hotfixes need a post-change review within 48 hours.
Rollback plans are mandatory for high-risk releases.

3. Access Control
Privileged production access is time-bound and ticketed.
Shared admin accounts are prohibited.
Access reviews for critical systems run quarterly.

4. Data Retention
Operational logs are retained for 90 days.
Customer data deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

5. Vendor Management
New SaaS tools require security review before purchase.
Contracts above £10,000 need Finance and Legal approval.
